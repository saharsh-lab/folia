              Method

              From photo to prescription

              1. 01

                Upload

                A single leaf photo is sent to the Flask backend. Nothing is saved to disk.

              2. 02

                Preprocess

                The image is converted to RGB and resized to 224×224 — exactly as in training.

              3. 03

                Classify

                A MobileNetV2 backbone, pretrained on ImageNet and fine-tuned on PlantVillage, scores all 38 classes.

              4. 04

                Advise

                The top prediction is paired with its cause, severity and treatment, alongside the next four candidates.

              Know the limits

              • Trained on lab photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds. Expect lower accuracy on field photos with clutter, shadows or several leaves.
              • Some conditions are harder than others — see accuracy by condition.
              • A decision aid, not a replacement for an expert diagnosis.
